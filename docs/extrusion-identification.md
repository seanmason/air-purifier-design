# Identify the available 2020 extrusion

2020 normally describes a 20 × 20 mm outside section. It does not uniquely identify the slot shape, T-nut size or screw thread.

Update 2026-10-27: user measurements are **6.28 mm mouth, 11 mm cavity width, 6.2 mm total depth and approximately 5.7 mm floor width**. The rectangular v01 carriers failed physical fit. The closest published comparison is Wellste W-6-2020 (6.2 / 11 / 6.1 mm); this is a likely family match, not confirmed brand identification. Sloped inner corners do not by themselves imply V-slot. Exact lip depth and slope angle remain unconfirmed. [Revised chamfered carriers and source comparison](../design/nut-fit-v02/README.md) use thin M5 nuts and four fit variants.

1. Look for the supplier, receipt, package label or part number. An exact profile drawing is the best reference.
2. Measure the outside width and height with calipers.
3. Measure the narrow slot opening across its parallel lips using the inside jaws. Avoid measuring a chamfer or the wider cavity underneath. Check several points.
4. Inspect a cut end. Record cavity width, lip thickness, slot depth and center-hole diameter if accessible. A square-on end photo beside a ruler and a slot close-up help identify the shape.
5. Note whether the slot entrance has pronounced V-shaped running surfaces or a conventional T-slot. Some nuts fit several profiles, but that does not establish bracket or printed-part compatibility.
6. If existing nuts fit, record their type and thread. Hand-tighten a sample nut and bracket to verify that it seats under the lips and the screw clamps before bottoming out.

For example, [MISUMI Series 5](https://sg.misumi-ec.com/pdf/fa/2013/p1839.pdf) has a 6 mm slot opening and supports several nut thread sizes. The number in the series name is not necessarily the slot width. Do not infer M5 threads from a 5 or 6 mm opening.

Send the measured opening and any supplier/part number first. If the profile remains unclear, compare an end-on photo with the manufacturer's drawing. We will keep printed slot fits and T-nut selection provisional until this is known.

For the purifier, favor metal brackets and matched T-nuts for structural connections. Printed parts should locate against known external faces where practical, reducing dependence on an unidentified internal slot profile.
